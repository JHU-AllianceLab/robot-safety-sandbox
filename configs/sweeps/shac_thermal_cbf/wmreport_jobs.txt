# The WM-filter report of the temperature-barrier SHAC (configs/sweeps/shac_thermal_cbf/
# jobs.txt): configs/sweeps/shac_noheight/wmreport_jobs.txt verbatim but for the policies

# with its heat model and the 70 C temperature barrier below EVERY controller (SONIC, pi,

# with a condition-independent key (every condition sees the same). The report adds, per
# filter, the share of motor-steps the barrier clipped and of episodes whose hottest
# winding still went over 70 C.

# then the report (job 10):
